Engo v17 — arrow placement and Arabic settings slider direction

1. Return-to-open-files arrow
- The arrow icon artwork is not mirrored or replaced between languages.
- Arabic: icon is placed on the right side of the label.
- English: icon is placed on the left side of the label.
- The behavior is applied both during initial UI creation and language changes.

2. Settings sliders (interface scale and font sizes)
- English: normal left-to-right progression.
- Arabic: visual slider progression is inverted, so the accent begins on the
  right and grows toward the left as the numeric value increases.
- The slider widget layout stays left-to-right, and the fixed endpoint labels
  are not reordered or repositioned.
- The stored values, ranges, and settings keys are unchanged.
